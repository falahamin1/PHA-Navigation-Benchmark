Physics-integrator confound — sim_violation rate in final greedy eval

A hard-coded assertion in nav_env.py (adjacency check on cell transitions) was firing and crashing training on HARD-tier jobs in the first batch — see callout above. It was patched to a caught, counted outcome (sim_violation, scored as not-solved) rather than a fixed dt/drift_coupling recalibration. That keeps training alive but means a slice of HARD-tier episodes are auto-scored as failures for a physics-engine reason, not a policy-quality reason. It's a small effect at final convergence (shown below) but not zero, and it lands unevenly across encoders.

Parameter counts — fairness check across encoders

DESIGN.md §1.7 asks for encoder parameter counts to be matched "within a small factor" so a solve-rate gap can't be explained by one model just being bigger. Counted directly from each encoder's saved checkpoint (model_state) rather than reconstructed — this part of the sweep is in good shape.
